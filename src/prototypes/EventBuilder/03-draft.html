Overview

Overview

The longest-running recognition programme in UK payments

Established in 2005, the Card & Payments Awards celebrate the organisations and individuals shaping how Britain pays. Over twenty-two years the programme has become the benchmark the industry measures itself against.

Judging is independent and confidential. Entries are assessed by a panel drawn from across the sector — issuers, networks, acquirers, fintechs and independent consultants — none of whom judge a category they compete in.

Giving back

21 years of charitable support

Over £525,000 raised to date through the awards dinner.

Each year the ceremony supports a charity chosen by the organising committee. The 2026 beneficiary was Food Bank Aid, which supplies a network of food banks across north London. The 2027 partner will be announced with the shortlist.

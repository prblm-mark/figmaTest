SeatingHeader

The Seating Planner's event bar, room selector and toolbar. Tier: Pattern. Composes Button, Toggle (toggle--xs, label-less) and RoomCard — one per plan, in the Room-Selector-Bar slot.

Figma calls this component Header. It is filed as SeatingHeader because src/patterns/Header is a different, unrelated component (the AI-Chat header).

The Type axis is data, not CSS: No Plans is the event bar on its own and Has Plans adds the room selector and the toolbar, so which one renders is markup the caller supplies. There are no Type modifiers — the fifth component in this module where that holds.

Many plans — the room selector scrolls

Figma's own mobile variant overflows its frame with two cards, so more plans than fit is the drawn state. The bar scrolls horizontally rather than wrapping, and shows no scrollbar — which is what Figma draws. Drag it with the mouse (the cursor turns to a grab handle only when there is somewhere to go), swipe it on a touch screen, or just Tab through the cards and let focus pull them into view. A drag that ends on a card deliberately does not activate it.

768–1023px — the toolbar stays compact

Not a Figma variant. The toolbar carries far more than the bars above it — room name, count, the switch, a divider, three buttons and the kebab — so it runs out of room long before the event bar does. It therefore keeps its compact form (stacked room label, no toggle or divider, two buttons) all the way to 1023px, while the event bar is already back to its desktop row. 1024px is the Seating Planner shell's own layout boundary — the prototype switches to its worklist at the same width. The toolbar's padding deliberately stays with its siblings, so all three regions keep a common inline padding.

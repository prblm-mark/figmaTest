TableListing

The Seating Planner tables grid and its toolbar. Tier: Pattern. Composes TableCard (one per table), Toggle (toggle--xxs) and Input (input--sm, label-less). Presentational only — filtering, search and the free-seats toggle are the parent module's job, and there is no JS here.

The grid is repeat(auto-fill, minmax(var(--ai-size-4), 1fr)) rather than Figma's literal four columns. TableCard has a 240px min-width, so four fixed columns need ~984px and would overflow between 768px and about 1000px; auto-fill lands on 4 columns at Figma's drawn width and 1 at mobile, stepping through 3 and 2 in between. Resize this page to watch the column count change.

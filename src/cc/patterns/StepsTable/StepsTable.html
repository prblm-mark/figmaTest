1 Agent Access - Let Trusted AI Agents Operate Affino for You, in the Browser and Beyond ContentNone Markus Karlsson 20 Jul 2026
  • No step image
  • No presentation image
  • No multimedia

The headline of 9.0.11.24 - and the reason it is The Autonomy Update - is Agent Access: trusted AI agents can now operate Affino for you, signing in and working across your sites and your platform without a person having to drive every step.

Operate your sites in the browser, as a real member

Agent Access issues a genuine, signed, single-use member login, so an agent can sign in to your site and move through it exactly as a real member would - browsing, navigating, and completing member journeys in a real browser. Page views, journeys, and conversions behave just as they do for a signed-in person, which makes it ideal for automating and verifying live journeys end to end.

Work across your platform too, alongside the toolkit you already have

Browser operation sits alongside the programmatic control Affino already offers. The Affino MCP toolkit lets agents manage the platform itself - CRM, content, media, forums and more - as an attributed member of your team, and the Zapier integration moves data in and out across thousands of apps.

Works with your preferred agent tools

Agent Access is open to any capable agent client, so your team can use whatever they already work with - Claude Cowork, Codex, the ChatGPT app, or an equivalent agent platform - to reach directly into Affino.

Locked down, layer by layer

Agent Access is built to be secured tightly, and the controls stack. You combine an Access Profile and a Bot Access Profile - which can restrict an agent by IP address or user agent - with Agent Access Keys that carry the credential and can be rotated or revoked.

2 Awards & Events - A Smoother Run From Entry to Judging to Event Day ContentNone Markus Karlsson 20 Jul 2026
  • No step image
  • No presentation image
  • No multimedia

Awards and events get a substantial operational lift in 9.0.11.24, making the whole cycle - from entry, through judging, to the event itself - smoother to run and better presented to entrants and attendees.

Cleaner judging

Shortlist judging now has its own dedicated route, so you can send judges straight to the right stage without shortlist and final judging blurring together. Rounds stay distinct, and judges see exactly the screen they need.

What's included:

  • Shortlist judging screen - judges can be sent to a dedicated shortlist judging screen.
  • Award introduction categories - Awards can show category lists, expandable detail, breakpoint columns, and entry prices.
  • Category icons - award categories can carry icons on the introduction screen.
